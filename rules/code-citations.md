# Code and standard citations

For substantive code/standard references, identify the original publication, actual edition/version, section/table/page and retrieval date. Link the original when accessible; for authorized supplied/licensed documents give a precise file/publication locator. Use the title and identifier shown by the publisher rather than a locally maintained abbreviation dictionary.

Formatting examples are placeholders only: `{code title} {edition} §{section}` or `{standard identifier}:{year}`. They do not select an edition, claim adoption or establish a requirement. Verify jurisdiction, date, adoption and amendments from the original for the actual task. Source manifests provide navigation only.

State the limited result of the performed analysis and unresolved applicability or evidence gaps. Do not infer compliance from a citation, data lookup or mechanical check. See [source navigation](../corpus/sources/catalog.json) and [completion reporting](../docs/completion-reporting.md).
