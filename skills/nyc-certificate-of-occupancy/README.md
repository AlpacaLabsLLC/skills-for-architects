# /as:nyc-certificate-of-occupancy

Retrieve NYC Certificate of Occupancy evidence for an identified property.

## Usage

`$nyc-certificate-of-occupancy certificate of occupancy or legal use by floor` on Codex; `/as:nyc-certificate-of-occupancy certificate of occupancy or legal use by floor` on Claude Code.

Input: Address and borough or BIN/BBL; certificate/date if known. Output: Property-specific occupancy-record evidence. Read-only; no studio setup required. Access, edition and applicability limitations are reported rather than replaced with model-memory answers.

Does not: Infer legal occupancy from PLUTO, a permit, or absence of a search result.
