# /as:nyc-local-law

Find an identified NYC Local Law and its enactment/effective-date evidence.

## Usage

`$nyc-local-law Local Law number and year` on Codex; `/as:nyc-local-law Local Law number and year` on Claude Code.

Input: Law number and year or unambiguous title. Output: Identified Local Law evidence. Read-only; no studio setup required. Access, edition and applicability limitations are reported rather than replaced with model-memory answers.

Does not: Assume enactment date is effective date or resolve all amendments.
