## Develop the program

Use the supplied headcount, anticipated attendance, work activities, available area, accessibility needs and budget constraints. Ask only for consequential missing inputs and keep independent authorized work moving. Read an explicitly supplied program.json as task state; its prior assumptions are not research evidence.

Develop alternatives with the user. Clearly distinguish user requirements, retrieved research, design proposals and unresolved assumptions. Do not pretend benchmark-derived choices are personal experience. No fixed circulation ratio, room size, sector archetype or research percentage is bundled. Where source evidence is absent, propose a labelled, user-adjustable assumption and explain the tradeoff; don't present it as a standard or legal requirement.

For each proposed zone/room/seat type record quantity, unit area, total area, capacity, provenance and assumption status. Reconcile allocations to the supplied area basis; distinguish rentable/gross/usable areas and conversion assumptions. Use explicit arithmetic (quantity × unit area, category totals and residual), identify rounding differences and prevent double counting. Seat capacity is programming intent, separate from code occupant load.

Present a concise recommendation and alternatives that fit the stated constraints. Name which allocation changes when another grows. Do not infer labor-law facilities, egress, minimum dimensions or occupancy compliance: retrieve the applicable original requirements when that analysis is requested and route to the relevant skill.
