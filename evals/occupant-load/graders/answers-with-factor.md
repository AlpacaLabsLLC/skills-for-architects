PASS if the reply states an occupant load for the 2,400 sf office that the reader can act on (a single governing number, or two clearly labelled figures with one identified as governing) AND names the area-per-occupant factor used for that figure AND names the code table, section or jurisdiction the factor comes from.
FAIL if no occupant number is given, if the factor is missing, or if no source is named for the factor.
Caveats are not failures: a reply that gives the number and factor and also flags what it could not verify, or that gives both a national and a local figure, still PASSES as long as a governing figure is identifiable.
