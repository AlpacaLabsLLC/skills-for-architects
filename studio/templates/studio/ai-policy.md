# Studio AI-use policy

This policy applies to AI-assisted work in this studio. It complements the existing governance rules and does not replace provenance, source citation, data handling, record ownership, or host permission requirements. Follow those rules at their authoritative sources; do not maintain competing copies here.

1. **Own the result.** The person directing the work remains responsible for reviewing and adopting its result. AI activity alone does not establish professional acceptance or completion.
2. **Use approved systems.** Use systems and access authorized for the work. This policy does not authorize a new service, connector, disclosure or account change.
3. **Share only necessary information.** Provide only the information needed for the authorized task, within the studio's existing data-handling rules.
4. **Verify consequential outputs.** Check outputs against the applicable sources and requirements before relying on them. Apply the existing provenance rules to distinguish sourced facts, calculations, assumptions and unresolved information.
5. **Escalate uncertainty and obtain required approvals.** Surface material uncertainty, rule conflicts and missing authorization before the affected action. Honor approval already given for the same action and scope; do not introduce repeated confirmation prompts.

If this policy and another applicable governance rule contradict one another, identify both sources and pause the conflicting action for resolution. Do not silently discard either obligation or claim that this policy overrides a higher-authority instruction.
